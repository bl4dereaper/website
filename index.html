The Architect

Shrieve Industries is the personal engineering division of Tom Shrieve — an Archives Imaging Specialist by trade and a relentless builder by nature. From hardware development and 3D printing to music production and emergency medical services, Shrieve Industries operates at the intersection of technology, creativity, and field-readiness.

Whether it's writing code, designing circuit boards, patrolling the slopes at Lost Valley, or responding to emergencies, the mission is the same: build things that matter, solve problems that don't quit, and never stop iterating.
